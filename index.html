How it works

The dispatch line is open around the clock, including nights, weekends and holidays. Stoneham

Water damage in town, in plain terms

Stoneham sits in Middlesex County with Spot Pond and the Middlesex Fells Reservation on one side and Interstate 93 and Route 28 running through. Those are the roads crews use to reach Montvale Avenue, Stoneham Center and the streets around Stoneham High School, so a call from anywhere in town is a short trip from our base at 2 Main St.

Public Works helps with what happens outside your house. The Stoneham Public Works Water/Sewer Department says it maintains about 75 miles of water mains and about 75 miles of sewer, with 5 sewer pumping stations, and that it works to cut sewer infiltration and inflow.

If water is coming up through a floor drain, call Public Works about the street side and call us about the damage inside. The town page says after-hours emergencies go to the Stoneham Police Department line.

Rebuilding can bring permits into the picture. The Stoneham Building Department at 35 Central Street takes applications through the town's online portal, and its page says to call the office for inspections. It does not say when water damage repair needs a permit, so ask before you close up a wall. For flood zones, check the FEMA flood map for your address instead of guessing.

Insurance

What to write down before the crew arrives

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by homeowners policies. Gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Read your policy or ask your insurer, because we cannot tell you what a policy pays.

Keep this for your claim

Photograph every wet room before anything is moved, note the date and time you found the water, keep damaged items until the adjuster says otherwise, and save every receipt. A large loss, such as water through two floors, gets a written scope and an assessment of the structure first.

FAQ

Questions before you call

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Stoneham?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day. Same-day response is available when a crew is available, and we do not promise an arrival time. While you wait, shut off the water source if you can reach it safely and keep people and pets out of wet or contaminated areas.

Is water damage restoration the same as basement waterproofing?

No. Restoration removes water, dries the structure and rebuilds. Waterproofing changes how water reaches the foundation, which is a different trade. If the same basement floods repeatedly, ask a waterproofing contractor about the cause after the damage is dried.

How long does drying take?

Most structures need several days of air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, and the crew checks moisture meter readings until materials return to normal. Thick framing and hardwood take longer than carpet pad and drywall.

What if the loss is large or covers more than one floor?

Call right away. A larger loss gets a walk-through, a written scope and a drying plan sized to the structure. Keep photos and receipts for your insurer.

How is price decided?

We explain the scope and what drives the price before work starts, and the dispatcher can talk through your situation by phone. Exact numbers depend on what the crew finds in the walls and floors.
